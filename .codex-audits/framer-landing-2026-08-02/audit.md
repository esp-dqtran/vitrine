# Framer landing-page clone feasibility audit

Date: 2026-08-02
Source: https://www.framer.com/
Capture sizes: 1440x1000 desktop, 390x844 mobile

## Verdict

Astryx can reproduce the page's layout system, motion quality, responsive behavior, and proof-led storytelling in the existing React/Vite stack. Rebuild it as clean Astryx components; do not copy Framer's generated DOM, proprietary font, copy, logos, customer marks, screenshots, or video assets.

## Captured steps

1. Desktop hero — healthy. Minimal navigation, a short value proposition, two CTAs, and one dominant animated product demo.
2. Agent feature story — healthy. Large real product media carries the message; copy stays subordinate.
3. Platform grid — healthy. A dense feature set becomes scannable through varied card sizes and real UI evidence.
4. Customer proof — healthy. Large, quiet story cards create trust without interrupting the visual rhythm.
5. Mobile hero — healthy. Typography reflows to 36/36, navigation collapses, heavy desktop media is reduced, and the page has no horizontal overflow.
6. Final conversion — healthy. The final CTA is a lightweight product interaction rather than another generic button band.

## What Astryx should adapt

- Keep the black editorial canvas, thin borders, compact navigation, large headings, and restrained CTAs.
- Replace the current decorative workflow previews with real Astryx captures: catalog search, evidence collection, live canvas collaboration, and a completed feature brief.
- Use one hero film to demonstrate the complete evidence-to-decision workflow.
- Recast the capability grid around Apps and Sites, Research Projects, Designer Canvas, Documents, Feature Briefs, and sharing.
- Use real catalog scale, public previews, and completed project outcomes as proof instead of borrowed customer logos.
- Preserve mobile media reduction and reduced-motion behavior.

## Risks and limits

- The Framer homepage is generated by Framer's runtime and uses a proprietary GT Walsheim font. Its generated DOM and minified runtime should not be copied.
- Five video elements and extensive transform-driven presentation were observed on desktop; their full reduced-motion behavior was not verified.
- The visible mobile menu was not represented as a named button in the accessibility snapshot and needs keyboard/assistive-technology testing.
- A heading jumps from H3 to H6 in the agent section. Gray-on-black copy also needs measured contrast testing.
- Screenshots alone do not prove full WCAG compliance or expose every hover, focus, and menu state.
